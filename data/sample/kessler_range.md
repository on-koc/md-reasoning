# The Kessler Range

The Kessler Range is a mountain chain in northern Valdoria. Its largest glacier, the
Brannoch Glacier, feeds the Ostra River, which supplies drinking water to Halvik.

Measurements from the Frostline Survey showed that the Brannoch Glacier lost about
twelve percent of its volume between 2014 and 2019. These findings prompted the
Valdorian Water Authority to fund new monitoring work by the Aurora Consortium.

In 2022 the Aurora Consortium installed automatic weather stations along the Ostra
River. Data from the stations is combined with Skylark imagery to forecast spring
meltwater floods in Halvik.
