# The Aurora Consortium

The Aurora Consortium is a research alliance founded in 2019 by the Veltran Institute
and Northwind University. Its goal is to build open tools for monitoring glaciers in
the Kessler Range. The consortium is headquartered in the city of Halvik.

Dr. Mira Okafor, a glaciologist at Northwind University, serves as the scientific
director of the Aurora Consortium. She previously led the Frostline Survey, a
five-year field campaign that mapped ice loss on the Kessler Range.

The Veltran Institute provides the consortium with its satellite imaging platform,
called Skylark. Skylark was designed by engineer Tomas Reyes, who joined the
Veltran Institute in 2016.
