# People of the Aurora Consortium

Tomas Reyes and Mira Okafor co-authored the 2021 paper "Seeing Ice from Orbit",
which describes how Skylark measures glacier thickness. The paper won the Halden
Prize for environmental engineering.

Lena Brandt is a hydrologist at the Valdorian Water Authority. She works with Mira
Okafor to translate glacier measurements into flood warnings for the Ostra River
valley. Lena Brandt also advises the city council of Halvik on water policy.

Northwind University hosts the consortium's data archive, which is maintained by
data engineer Samuel Ito.
